Who we are

Equipping young people to shape their own future.

The Reform and Rehabilitation Foundation (RRF) is being established to contribute to the holistic development of youth. Recognizing the unique challenges faced by young individuals, RRF aims to bridge gaps in career guidance, skill development, and community resilience through knowledge, training, research, and advocacy interventions.

We believe that with the right support and guidance, the youth can achieve their full potential and contribute to the socio-economic growth of society at large.

“Creating pathways where ambition meets the right opportunity.”

Vision

To create a thriving environment where young individuals in Jammu & Kashmir have access to the right tools and opportunities for personal and professional growth, leading them to skilled, self-reliant individuals and valuable contributors to society.

Mission

To create an employable workforce by bridging the gap between ambition and opportunity through targeted career guidance, skill development, counseling, mentorship, and rehabilitation programs, creating pathways for sustainable development in alignment with the National Education Policy 2020 and Sustainable Development Goals.

Our work

Key programs

Evidence-led interventions designed to improve access, agency and opportunity for young people, students, artisans and communities.

01

Strengthening Career Guidance Ecosystem in J&K

System-level work with education authorities to institutionalize career guidance resources, tools and training across the school ecosystem.

  • 20 district and 2 divisional-level offline consultations with UNICEF & J&K Government.
  • 500+ government officials oriented and sensitized.
  • 9,000+ formally designated Career Coordinators oriented and trained.
  • STEM Career Handbook developed to address gender disparity in STEM.
02

Promoting STEM Careers among Girls

School-based workshops encouraging girls to explore STEM pathways and make career choices based on interests and aptitudes.

  • 40 sessions conducted across Jammu & Kashmir.
  • 2,733 students participated.
  • Focused on stereotypes, women role models and informed career choices.
03

Lodha Genius Scholarship Programme

Promoting the Ashoka University Lodha Genius STEM Scholarship Programme with district education authorities.

  • 3,700+ students from J&K enrolled.
  • 7 students qualified for the scholarship.
  • Ridhakshi Ganjoo from Pulwama secured a fully funded Dubai opportunity in 2024.
04

Scientific Career Decision-Making

Knowledge interventions exposing students to psychometric realities and labour-market indicators for data-driven career planning.

  • Government schools across 10 districts of Kashmir.
  • Implemented with IIEDC, NIT Srinagar and Move Beyond.
  • 1,000+ students reached through awareness workshops.
05

District Srinagar Livelihood Need Assessment

An evidence-based assessment designed to inform targeted livelihood-generation interventions across all 7 tehsils of Srinagar.

  • 100 youth and 100 households assessed.
  • 8 Focus Group Discussions conducted with 64 artisans.
  • Coverage across Central Shalteng, Chanapora/Natipora, Eidgah, Khanyar, Pantha Chowk, Srinagar North and Srinagar South.
06

Integrated District Development Program

Career guidance and counselling, general entrepreneur training, Futurepreneur, ONDC onboarding and social-security access.

  • 200+ government higher secondary students trained in career guidance and 21st-century skills.
  • 500+ government college students engaged through entrepreneurship programmes.
  • 250+ artisans engaged for ONDC onboarding.
  • 600+ low-income households enabled to access relevant welfare schemes.
07

Student Engagement & Career Development

Ongoing youth and career development interventions using individualized counselling, mentor-connect programmes, psychometric assessments and expert lectures.

  • 30,000+ students engaged to date.
08 · SIGNATURE ECOSYSTEM INTERVENTION

Cyber Awareness cum Hygiene Programme · UT of J&K

In collaboration with I3C-IIT Jammu, RRF is implementing a UT-wide Cyber Awareness cum Hygiene Programme across all 20 districts of Jammu & Kashmir, with administrative support from the Office of the Hon’ble Lieutenant Governor of J&K and collaboration across government departments and educational institutions.

  • 20,000+ individuals have completed Cyber First Responder training as of August 2026.
  • 200,000+ individuals engaged and sensitized through cyber awareness sessions.
  • Institutional partnerships span education, skill development, rural development, social welfare, universities, colleges and schools.
  • Network includes government and private schools, PM SHRI schools, Police Public Schools, Army Public and Goodwill Schools and Waqf Schools.
